Calm & Clear Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Calm & Clear ("the app," "we," "us") is a mindfulness and study-habit app built by GMRT Apps. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Who can use this app

Calm & Clear is intended for users age 13 and older. The app asks for a date of birth on first launch and blocks anyone who indicates they're under 13.

Information we collect

When you use Calm & Clear, the app stores the following in your own account, hosted on Supabase (our backend provider):

  • Mood and stress check-ins — the mood or stress level you select, and the date/time
  • Session activity — breathing session length, Pomodoro focus sessions, and which tools you've used (Basics lessons, Reframing exercises, Goals)
  • Reflection journal entries and Reframing exercise text — whatever you choose to write
  • Goals you create and mark complete
  • Optional profile info — a display name and date of birth, if you choose to provide them
  • Zip code (optional) — only if you use the Environment feature, to look up local weather/air-quality conditions
  • Account identifier — the app creates an anonymous account for you automatically; if you choose to sign up with an email and password, that's stored for login purposes only

We do not require an account, name, or contact information to use the app's core features.

How your information is used

Your data is used only to run the app's own features for you: showing your mood/stress trends, your streaks and growth level, your saved journal entries, and your Environment weather card. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Analytics

The app includes an analytics system that can log which features are used (for example, "a breathing session was completed" or "dark mode was turned on"). These events carry only structural information — counts, durations, and fixed categories like mood type — never your journal text, thoughts, birth date, or name. Any user identifier included is a one-way cryptographic hash, not your actual account ID.

Data deletion

You can delete all of your logged activity at any time from Account → Delete My Data, or permanently delete your account and all associated data from Account → Delete Account. Account deletion cannot be undone.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider, using row-level security so that only your own account can read or write your data. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Children's privacy

Calm & Clear is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. The app's sign-up flow is designed to block access for users who indicate an age under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
